Luke (@vikingarmory2a) creates tactical and firearms content focused on gear that holds up outside the box — EDC setups, range sessions, home-defense loadouts, and honest breakdowns of what's actually worth carrying.

The audience is engaged and gear-literate: people who watch a full review before they buy, ask follow-up questions in the comments, and trust a recommendation because it's been tested, not just unboxed.
